A letter for a very dear friend

To My Dearest Friend,

On the occasion of your twenty-second year.

First of all Happy Birthday Kuttaa. 22 vayas aayi prayam aayi varuva health okke nookenam. 6 varsham aayi anne parichayapettitt but still you keep amazing me with your worderful mandatherams. Orupaad potta decisions eduthitund nee but choosing me as your friend was not among them. You are blessed to have me as your friend. Soulmatesill Toxon parayane poole tapas erunnal kittuvo enne poole oru kidiliam friendine.

Nammal angane adhikkam samsarikkar onnum ellengilum you will always be my best friend, never once regretted for choosing you as my best friend. Nthooram paradhooshanam aa nammal parayane. Ath oru vibe thanne antte koode parayan. Edekk edekk njn oro loopil pett pokumbol aa message ayikkan okke marakkunne pakshe marakkand reel ayikkum .Enakk aa munjiya schoolil ninn aake kittiya anneyaa ath kond mathram aa avide chernath regret cheyyathe. Enne patti ee lokath ettavum kooduthal ariyavunne ninakkaa athond anne angne veruthe vidan ulla plan ella You know too much. Pinne 22 vayass aayi prayam aayi enn okke vicharich valli pidikkunath kurakkaruth nallonam valli pidikkenam enitt orooo preshnathil chenn chaadenam.

Enikk engane letter ezhuthi onnum sheelam ella. Athukond enna pareyenam enn areella. Eniyum njn ezhutiyal veruthe over aaki kulavakkum so eth evide neruthuvaaa. Nthayalum nee eth prethekshichu kanilla enn enikk urappa.

But i am genuinely happy for you kutta. Oru vettam koode Happy Birthday and I never ever regretted having you as my best friend NEERKOLI.

With affection, gratitude, and the warmest wishes,

[Shotta]
